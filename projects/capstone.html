Pinch-Free Animatronic Shells

Project Background

This project is focused on the design and development of pinch-free animatronic shells. In many animatronic systems, moving parts can create pinch points that are dangerous for guests. Our goal is to create a shell system that eliminates these hazards while maintaining the required range of motion and aesthetic quality of the robot arm.

My Contributions

I initially had the idea of starting a capstone project with WDI when I was interning there, so I connected the relevant people at WDI with UCSB's capstone team to get one started.
My primary technical contributions to this project include the mechanical design of the tooth and neck pinch prevention, and the animation of the robot using ROS2 and the Dynamixel SDK. I'm also handling the heat dissipation testing of our motors to make sure our pinch solutions don't end up overheating the robot.

Lessons Learned

As this project has continued, I've gained valuable experience in:

  • Exporting URDF files from SolidWorks
  • Integration of soft materials in robotic systems (urethane foam casting for the teeth)
  • Iterative design and testing for complex mechanical assemblies
