Anchor Launcher

Project Background

This was the first research project I worked on in the Hawkes Lab. Lunar and Martian rovers can get entrapped at the bottom of steep slopes or in soft sand, so we designed a system to allow them to independently rescue themselves by launching and then winching on an anchor. In the video, we demonstrate a 2 degree increase in slope climbing ability on a simple rover platform at NASA's Glenn Research Center.

My Contributions

My primary contributions to this project were the design of the anchor and the clutch mechanism that we used to winch the anchor. I also first authored the paper we wrote on the system, and presented it at ICRA 2025 in Atlanta!
