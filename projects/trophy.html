Ping Pong Trophy

Project Background

During my internship at Imagineering, us interns threw a ping pong competition between the Ride and R&D Departments. Mine and my friend's task was to design and build the trophy, but we only had 24 hours to work on it.
Using a laser cutter, scrap material, and some LED lights we found, we managed to put together a pretty solid trophy - and we also won it back for R&D!

My Contributions

My primary contributions to this project were assembling the electronics, and designing and making the lightsaber-style handle of the trophy!

I'm still waiting on a group picture from this day.
For now, imagine a bunch of people smiling while holding the trophy!

Lessons Learned

This project allowed me to practice several skills such as:

  • Design for aesthetics, as opposed to function
  • Soldering
  • Spray painting
