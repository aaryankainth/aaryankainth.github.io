Hexagonal Shelves

Project Background

My roommate (pictured in the first image) and I thought our walls looked a little bland. We wanted to make something to spice things up a bit, so over winter break our sophomore year, we made a set of six hexagonal shelves to split between us!

Lessons Learned

From this project, I got better at using a miter saw, as well as learned more on the importance of planning. The lesson about planning was almost learned the hard way, when we realized that we had been cutting the wrong angle (60 instead of 30 degrees) into the wood! Luckily, we realized very quickly, and were still able to make all the shelves as we wanted.
